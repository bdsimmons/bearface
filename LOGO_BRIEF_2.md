# Bearface logo — round 2

Round 1 (in `logo-proposals/`) was rejected by the owner, hard. What failed:
all three marks were blocky and crude — cartoon front-facing faces, chunky
low-poly shapes, mascot energy. They looked assembled from rectangles, not
drawn by a designer.

An image of the ORIGINAL 2016 Bearface mark is attached: a bear head in side
profile, carved out of an angular badge in negative space. Study it. It is
more elegant than everything from round 1 — a single confident silhouette,
the bear formed by what's cut away. Its only flaws: the curves are a bit soft
and organic for the site's technical aesthetic, and detail is lost at 24px.

## The task

Three new candidates, replacing the old files:

- `logo-proposals/candidate-a.svg` — a faithful MODERNIZATION of the original
  badge: same concept (side-profile head, negative space, angular badge),
  redrawn with tighter, more deliberate geometry so it feels precise and
  holds at 24px. This is the priority candidate.
- `logo-proposals/candidate-b.svg` — a different elegant take on a bear in
  side profile using negative space. No badge required.
- `logo-proposals/candidate-c.svg` — one wildcard, your call — but it must
  pass the same taste bar.

Update `logo-proposals/NOTES.md` for the new candidates.

## The taste bar (non-negotiable)

- Side profile or abstract silhouette only. NO front-facing faces. No eyes
  drawn as blobs, no ears as circles, nothing cute, nothing mascot-like.
- Think of the calibre of mark a serious brand studio ships: one idea,
  executed with restraint — closer to the WWF panda, the NBC peacock's
  discipline, or the original mark itself than to an icon-pack glyph.
- Few shapes, deliberate angles, consistent optical weight. If a path looks
  like it was snapped to a coarse grid, redraw it.
- Same technical constraints as before: ~0 0 100 100 viewBox, every fill
  `currentColor`, no gradients, no text, legible at 24px, strong at 200px.
- Do not modify theme files — proposals only.
